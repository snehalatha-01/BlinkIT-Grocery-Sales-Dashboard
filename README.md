# BlinkIT-Grocery-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive Excel dashboard and analytics project analyzing 8,523 BlinkIT grocery sales records across product categories, outlet types, and location tiers. It translates raw sales data into key business metrics, statistical insights, and executive visualizations to drive retail strategy.
BlinkIT Sales Analysis Dashboard 🛒📊An interactive, data-driven Excel dashboard created to analyze BlinkIT's sales performance, product visibility, outlet distribution, and key statistical insights across 8,523 transaction records.This project translates raw retail and quick-commerce sales data into actionable business intelligence to optimize category management, evaluate outlet effectiveness, and enhance inventory strategies.📌 Project Overview & ObjectivesAs a Data Analyst, the primary objective of this project is to evaluate BlinkIT's operational and sales performance across different product categories, fat content profiles, outlet types, and location tiers.The analysis is structured into four core business objectives:1. Overall Sales PerformanceCalculate Total Sales Revenue generated across all outlets.Compute the Average Sales Value per item record.Evaluate the Average Shelf/App Visibility of products across inventory.2. Product Category & Sales ComparisonAnalyze the revenue share of the Top 4 Product Categories vs. remaining categories.Identify the highest and lowest-performing product categories.Compare total sales volume across different outlet channels (Supermarket Type1, Type2, Type3, and Grocery Stores).3. Outlet Performance & Sales RelationshipsContrast sales trends between Low Fat and Regular products across outlet types.Investigate the relationship/correlation between Product Visibility and Sales.Assess how product category contributions vary across different outlet types.4. Statistical Analysis of SalesDetermine key statistical distribution measures: Mean, Median, Minimum, Maximum, Standard Deviation, and IQR.Identify potential sales outliers using statistical bounds ($1.5 \times \text{IQR}$).Evaluate average sales performance across outlet locations (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3 cities).📈 Key Insights & Executive Summary💰 Total Revenue: $1,201,681.49 generated across 8,523 product records with an average item revenue of $140.99.🏆 Top Revenue Categories: The Top 4 categories contribute 50.60% ($608,093.41) of overall sales revenue:Fruits and Vegetables: $178,124.08 (14.82%)Snack Foods: $175,433.92 (14.60%)Household: $135,976.53 (11.32%)Frozen Foods: $118,558.88 (9.87%)🏪 Primary Outlet Driver: Supermarket Type1 is the dominant revenue generator, contributing 65.54% ($787,549.89) of total sales.🥗 Fat Content Preference: Low Fat products drive 62.85% ($755,302.26) of sales, significantly outperforming Regular items ($446,379.23) across all channels.👁️ Visibility vs. Sales Correlation: Correlation coefficient is -0.0013, indicating that product visibility alone does not drive sales volume; demand is heavily utility-driven.🏙️ Location Tier Performance: Tier 3 location outlets generate the highest overall sales revenue ($554,191.09), while Tier 1 leads in average transaction value per item ($142.77).📊 Dashboard Features & ArchitectureThe project is packaged in BlinkIT_Sales_Analysis_Dashboard.xlsx, which contains three structured worksheets:Dashboard Sheet:Executive KPI Cards: Quick visual metrics for Total Sales, Average Sales, Product Count, Average Visibility, and Low Fat Share.Interactive Embedded Charts:Pie Chart: Top 4 vs. Remaining Categories Sales Share.Grouped Column Chart: Outlet Performance by Product Fat Content.Horizontal Bar Chart: Comprehensive 16-Category Sales Breakdown.Column Chart: Average Revenue Performance by Location Tier.Analysis & Pivot Summaries Sheet:Fully automated summary tables using Excel dynamic formulas (SUMIF, SUMIFS, AVERAGE, MEDIAN, STDEV.S, CORREL, QUARTILE.INC).Raw Data Sheet:Cleaned transaction records (8,523 items) with standardized data types and normalized category values.📂 Repository Structure├── BlinkIT_Sales_Analysis_Dashboard.xlsx   # Main Excel Workbook with Dashboard & Analysis
