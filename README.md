# 🛒 BlinkIT Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The BlinkIT Sales Analysis Dashboard is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze grocery sales performance and generate meaningful business insights.

The project uses the BlinkIT Grocery Dataset containing 8,523 records. It focuses on understanding sales trends, product performance, outlet characteristics, and customer purchasing patterns through data analysis and visualization.

An interactive-style dashboard was developed using Excel charts, formulas, and statistical metrics to present key performance indicators (KPIs) in an easy-to-understand format.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall grocery sales performance.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories.
- Compare sales across different outlet types.
- Understand the impact of fat content on sales.
- Analyze sales based on outlet location and size.
- Evaluate the relationship between product visibility and sales.
- Generate meaningful insights to support business decisions.

## 📊 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard highlights the following key performance indicators:

1. **Total Sales Revenue:** Measures the overall revenue generated from grocery products.
2. **Average Sales per Record:** Calculates the average sales value across all records.
3. **Average Product Visibility:** Measures the average visibility of products in outlets.
4. **Top 4 Category Share:** Represents the combined sales contribution of the four leading product categories.
5. **Visibility-Sales Correlation:** Measures the relationship between product visibility and sales.

## 📊 Excel Formulas Used

The following Excel functions were used to perform calculations and generate insights:

- `SUM()` – Calculates total sales.
- `AVERAGE()` – Calculates average sales.
- `MEDIAN()` – Finds the middle sales value.
- `SUMIF()` – Calculates sales based on a single condition.
- `SUMIFS()` – Calculates sales based on multiple conditions.
- `COUNTIF()` – Counts records based on conditions.
- `STDEV()` – Measures sales variation.
- `CORREL()` – Measures the relationship between two variables.
